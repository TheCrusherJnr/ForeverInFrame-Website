HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
Each ad below is ready to paste into Meta Ads Manager. The four
fields you need are:
  - Primary Text (the caption above the media)
  - Headline (bold text under the image/video)
  - Description (one grey line under the headline)
  - CTA Button (choose from Meta's preset list)

Run ads in GROUPS of 2-3 variants per audience so Meta's algorithm
can pick the winner. Swap creative every 10-14 days to avoid fatigue.

CREATIVE BEST PRACTICES
================================================================

1.  FIRST 3 SECONDS
    Meta gives you 3 seconds to hook a scroller. Best openings:
      - Bride's reaction during first look
      - Father-daughter vow moment
      - A quote overlaid on a beautiful clip
    AVOID establishing shots of venues — they don't stop thumbs.

2.  SUBTITLES ALWAYS
    85% of Meta video is watched sound-off. Burn captions into
    the video file (not Meta's auto-captions — they turn off).

3.  VERTICAL FIRST
    Export all video at 9:16 (1080×1920) for Reels/Stories.
    Create a 1:1 (1080×1080) version for Feed if budget allows.
    Don't upload horizontal — Meta downranks letterboxed content.

4.  DYNAMIC CREATIVE
    Upload 3-5 primary texts, 3-5 headlines, 3-5 videos/images
    per ad set. Let Meta auto-combine. Typically lifts CTR 15-30%
    vs fixed creative.

5.  FREQUENCY CAP
    Wedding audience is small and enthusiastic but fatigues fast.
    Hard cap at 3 impressions per week, or rotate creative every
    10-14 days.

6.  LANDING PAGE CONGRUENCE
    The ad promise must match what's visible above the fold on
    the landing page. Your hero says "Your wedding, as a film
    you'll watch forever" — use those exact words in at least
    one ad variant. Lifts conversion ~20%.

7.  PIXEL EVENTS TO TRACK
      - PageView              (all visitors)
      - ViewContent           (scrolled past hero)
      - InitiateCheckout      (clicked "Check my date")
      - Lead                  (form submitted)
      - Schedule              (consultation toggled)
    Optimise the campaign for Lead, not PageView.

8.  LEAD QUALIFICATION
    Including "from $3,199" in the ad copy filters budget-less
    tyre-kickers but cuts volume ~30%. Start WITHOUT price for
    first 7 days to gather data; add price only if form spam
    becomes a problem.

9.  COMMENTS ARE FREE SOCIAL PROOF
    Engaged couples READ comments before clicking. Reply to
    every comment within 4 hours (Meta rewards engagement).
    Ask past clients to comment "Loved working with Matthew!"
    on your top 1-2 ads.

10. WEEKDAYS 6-9PM
    Wedding research peaks weeknight evenings. If you're on a
    tight budget, set a custom delivery schedule for 6-9pm AEST
    Mon-Thu + all-day Sunday.

COMPLIANCE NOTES (Meta ad policies)
================================================================

DO NOT USE:
  - "You" / "your" pointing at physical traits, weight, illness
  - "Perfect day" / "dream wedding" - flagged as aspirational
  - Before/after comparisons (e.g. "with/without videography")
  - Testimonial screenshots with names + faces unless consented

SAFE PHRASING:
  "Brisbane couples have trusted me to film 50+ weddings"  ✓
  "Your wedding, as a film you'll watch forever"           ✓
  "This month, save $600"                                  ✓

MEDIA REQUIREMENTS:
  - Max 20% text on static images (soft rule, but affects reach)
  - No auto-playing audio in images
  - No misleading "Play" buttons on static images
  - No countdown timers inside the creative (use Meta's native
    end-date scheduling instead)
